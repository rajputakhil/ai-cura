Title: RNA sequencing confirms exon skipping and a frameshift for a COL1A1 intronic variant in a
family with osteogenesis imperfecta
Authors: I. Placeholder, J. Demonstration (Fictional Institute of Skeletal Genetics)
Recruitment: a single proband with a clinical diagnosis of osteogenesis imperfecta, referred for
genetic testing after a fracture history inconsistent with accidental trauma.

Target variant: NM_000088.4:c.588+5G>A

RNA STUDY
RNA was extracted from proband-derived skin fibroblasts and analyzed by RT-PCR followed by
Sanger sequencing of the resulting products, alongside RNA-seq for confirmation. The RT-PCR
products from the proband showed a shorter band than controls, consistent with skipping of the
adjacent exon. Sequencing confirmed complete skipping of exon 8 in the vast majority of transcript
reads (near-complete proportion), which removes 61 nucleotides and shifts the reading frame,
predicted to trigger nonsense-mediated decay (NMD) of the resulting transcript. Both proband and
unaffected control fibroblast RNA were analyzed in parallel using the same protocol.

No case-control cohort was assembled for this variant. No cosegregation data are available, as no
other family members were tested. No de novo status has been established, as parental samples
were not available. This single RNA-seq/RT-PCR study is the only functional evidence reported.
