Title: Case-control enrichment of a recurrent SCN5A missense variant in Brugada syndrome
Authors: A. Fictional, B. Placeholder (Institute of Demonstration Cardiogenetics)
Recruitment: 85 unrelated probands with a clinical diagnosis of Brugada syndrome were recruited
from a single arrhythmia referral center between 2016 and 2023. Diagnostic ajmaline challenge
testing was performed on all probands. Whole-exome sequencing was performed on all probands.

Target variant: NM_198056.3:c.5350G>A (p.Val1784Met)

CASE ENRICHMENT
The variant was identified in 9 unrelated probands in our Brugada syndrome cohort (9/85). It was
absent from 3,212 in-house control exomes from individuals without a personal or family history
of arrhythmia. Each of the 9 carriers was an independent proband from a distinct family; only the
proband was counted per family. The enrichment of this variant in cases versus controls was
statistically significant (p < 0.001).

No family members of any carrier were available for follow-up testing, so no segregation analysis
could be performed. No functional (electrophysiological) studies of this variant were undertaken
by this group or reported elsewhere. No RNA-based splicing study was performed for this variant.
Carriers presented with a range of arrhythmia phenotypes (syncope, documented VT, and asymptomatic
ECG findings on family screening for unrelated reasons); this phenotypic range is not considered
distinctive enough to be highly specific to SCN5A-related disease on its own.
