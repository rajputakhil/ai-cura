Title: Recurrent de novo SCN2A missense variant in three unrelated children with early-onset
developmental and epileptic encephalopathy
Authors: G. Fictional, H. Example (Center for Demonstration Pediatric Neurogenetics)
Recruitment: 3 unrelated probands with early-onset developmental and epileptic encephalopathy
were recruited via a pediatric neurogenetics clinic between 2019 and 2023. Trio (proband +
both biological parents) whole-exome sequencing was performed for all three families.

Target variant: NM_021007.3:c.2573G>A (p.Arg858Gln)

DE NOVO CONFIRMATION
The target variant was identified in all 3 probands. In each family, targeted Sanger sequencing
confirmed the variant was absent in both biological parents, and biological parentage was
confirmed by informative SNP analysis in all three trios. All three occurrences are therefore
independently confirmed de novo events. No parent in any family carried the variant, and none of
the parents reported a personal history of seizures or developmental delay.

No case-control cohort comparison was performed for this variant. No functional (electrophysiology)
studies were conducted. No RNA-based splicing study was performed. Phenotypes across the three
probands varied in severity and are not considered specific enough on their own, independent of
the de novo evidence, to support a phenotype-specificity claim.
