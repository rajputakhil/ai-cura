Title: Independent replication of an MYBPC3 missense variant in a European hypertrophic
cardiomyopathy cohort
Authors: M. Different, N. Separate, O. Independent (Northern European Cardiogenetics Consortium)
Recruitment: an independent cohort of 58 unrelated probands with hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM)
was recruited across three European centers between March 2015 and June 2021. This cohort has no
overlap with any previously published series. Whole-genome sequencing was performed.

Target variant: NM_000256.3:c.1504C>T (p.Arg502Trp)

CASE ENRICHMENT
The variant was found in 4 unrelated probands (4/58) and was absent from 5,120 in-house population
controls without cardiac disease. Each carrier was an independent proband from a distinct family.

SEGREGATION
In two multiplex families followed at our centers, the variant was tracked by Sanger sequencing and
cosegregated with genetically confirmed HCM in 5 additional affected relatives beyond the probands.
One obligate carrier was documented. No genetically confirmed carrier over age 45 was unaffected.

FUNCTIONAL STUDIES
Independently of prior work, we generated induced pluripotent stem cell–derived cardiomyocytes
(iPSC-CMs) from two carriers and isogenic controls. Compared with wild-type and a null (truncating)
control, the p.Arg502Trp cardiomyocytes showed impaired contractility and reduced myosin-binding
protein C incorporation. Both normal and abnormal controls were included. These assays were
performed by the study authors.

PHENOTYPE
All four carriers presented the characteristic HCM phenotype (asymmetric septal hypertrophy). The
genetic workup (WGS) excluded other sarcomeric-gene variants in all carriers.

No RNA-based splicing study was performed for this variant.
